About Ying Tang Hong
Investment

Founded in 1990 as an international trading company, we have evolved into a boutique private investment vehicle focused on long-term value creation across selected sectors and markets.

Ying Tang Hong helps international companies navigate opportunities in Greater China. For more than three decades, our investors, partners and operating teams have built trusted relationships with leading enterprises across Asia, Europe and North America — combining global perspective with on-the-ground expertise.

Today, YTH operates with patient capital and a long horizon. We back resilient businesses, exceptional operators and the structural shifts shaping the next generation of value across Greater China and beyond.

03 · Philosophy

A long horizon. A quiet
conviction. Disciplined capital.

We invest where capital, patience and partnerships compound. Our approach blends the discipline of a private investment office with the entrepreneurial instincts of a long-tenured operator.

We believe the best returns are earned over years, not quarters — and that the most durable businesses are built by operators who combine deep sector knowledge with the humility to learn from their markets.

  • 01
    Patient Capital We measure investment cycles in years and decades, allowing compounding to do its work without forced exit pressure.
  • 02
    Operator Partnerships We back exceptional operators and contribute networks, strategic counsel, and balance-sheet strength — not bureaucracy.
  • 03
    Selective Conviction A handful of high-conviction investments each year, chosen for resilience, governance, and the asymmetry of being right for a long time.
  • 04
    Stewardship & Trust Capital is a responsibility. We treat every investment as if it were our family's — with transparency, alignment and integrity.
